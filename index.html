Our Story & Community

Bringing Basic Education Closer to Home

Established on September 29, 2020, Upper Rancho Elementary School sits on a 2,051-square-meter campus in Purok 6, Sitio Upper Rancho, Barangay Bangahan, Pangantucan, Bukidnon. Operating as an extension school of Bangahan Integrated School, URES eliminates long, difficult commutes for young children in our farming community.

Surrounded by agricultural families cultivating corn, rice, sugarcane, coconut, and cassava, our school thrives through the strong bayanihan spirit of Ilonggo and Cebuano parents, Barangay Bangahan officials, the PTA, and local government partners.

School Profile at a Glance

  • Mother School: Bangahan Integrated School
  • Campus Area: 2,051 sq. meters
